Parts that are built from, or checked against, other projects
--------------------------------------------------------------

emu/bink.js, emu/bink_tables.js
    Bink 1 video and audio decoding. The bitstream handling follows the format as implemented in FFmpeg
    (libavcodec/bink.c, binkdsp.c, binkaudio.c); the tables are generated from FFmpeg's binkdata.h
    (tools/gen_bink_tables.js in the development tree).
    FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later. https://ffmpeg.org
    These two files are ordinary, readable JavaScript: you can read, change and replace them.
    The licence text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html

emu/smk.js
    Smacker video decoding (2.0.0). It follows the format as implemented in FFmpeg (libavcodec/smacker.c) and was checked frame by
    frame against FFmpeg's output; the same LGPL 2.1 or later note as above applies.

emu/mp3.js, emu/mp3tab.js
    MP3 (Layer III) decoding is a port of minimp3 by lieff, https://github.com/lieff/minimp3 , released under CC0 1.0
    (public domain dedication). No conditions; credit is given here.

emu/archives.js
    The reader for Wise installer programs. Its handling of the installer's file overlay was checked against the notes of the
    REWise project (GPL-3.0). No code from REWise is used here.
